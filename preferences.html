Email & notifications

Your notification preferences

Arkive sends very little. When a capsule is delivered to you, you'll get one email so you know it's arrived. If you've chosen to, you'll also get a quiet note when a capsule you sent is opened. Nothing else.

Where to change them

Every notification is controlled inside the Arkive app, on the device where you're signed in:

  1. Open Arkive on your iPhone.
  2. Go to your Profile.
  3. Open Notifications to turn each kind of email and alert on or off.

Prefer to stop delivery emails altogether?

Email support@arkive-it.com and we'll remove your address from capsule delivery emails. A real person reads that inbox.

This page is informational only — it can't see or change your account, and it never asks for your details. Manage everything from the app.
